Privacy notice

Last updated 18 November 2026

DirectQuotes is operated by Netic Technologies. This notice explains how we handle the details you submit on directquotes.co.za, in line with POPIA.

What we collect

Your name, phone number, email address, the address of the gate (including its map coordinates), and the details of your gate motor problem.

Why we collect it

To arrange repair quotes for you. With your consent, we share your details with multiple gate motor technicians near you, who may contact you.

Address search

When you type an address, the text you type is sent to Google Maps Platform so it can suggest matching addresses. Google handles that data under its own privacy policy.

Analytics and advertising

We use Google Analytics and Google Ads to measure how people find and use this site. These tools set cookies and receive usage data such as pages viewed and whether a quote request was submitted. They do not receive your contact details.

How long we keep it

Up to 12 months, then we delete it.

Your rights

You can ask to see, correct or delete your information at any time by emailing info@netictechnologies.com.

DirectQuotes is a service of Netic Technologies. We only use your details to arrange your quote.
